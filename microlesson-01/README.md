## What makes a task a good fit for GenAI?

Before you ask *“Can I use AI for this?”*, try asking:  
**“Is this the kind of thing GenAI is good at?”**

Here are some clues:

> 💡 **High-potential AI tasks often share these traits:**
>
> - They involve **language**, text, or structured patterns (e.g. reports, emails, code, summaries).
> - They are **repetitive or predictable**, but not purely mechanical (e.g. writing variations, analyzing tone).
> - They **require broad knowledge**, not deep domain expertise.
> - They benefit from a **fast first draft**, but still need review.
> - They can be **broken into smaller steps** with clear inputs and outputs.

> ⚠ **Low-potential or high-risk tasks may involve:**
>
> - Sensitive data (e.g. protected health info, customer PII)
> - Legal, financial, or safety-critical decisions
> - High ambiguity or open-ended judgment
> - Real-time or high-stakes consequences

## Individual vs. Organizational Opportunities

AI can help at many levels:

| Level | Examples of AI-Augmentable Tasks |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| **Individual** | Drafting emails, summarizing meetings, cleaning messy data, suggesting SQL queries |
| **Team**       | Automating weekly reports, synthesizing survey results, standardizing documentation |
| **Org-wide**   | Creating policy assistants, auto-tagging large datasets, triaging internal requests |

> 🧠 Think about your own day-to-day work. What’s repetitive? What takes time but little judgment? What’s buried in noisy text or spreadsheets?
