## Not All Tasks Are Meant for Full Automation

> 💡 The most effective AI workflows combine automation with human expertise. This is called **“human-in-the-loop.”**

AI can boost efficiency, but:
- Some decisions require human judgment or ethical consideration.
- Some steps need a person to check the output before it’s final.
- Some processes *shouldn’t* be automated at all.

---

### Signs a Task Should Stay Human-in-the-Loop

- High stakes: Legal, safety, or reputational consequences if something goes wrong.
- Ambiguous or nuanced: Needs context, empathy, or experience.
- Sensitive data: Personal information, regulated content, or confidential business info.
- Quality control: When accuracy and reliability *must* be validated.
